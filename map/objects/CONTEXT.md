# Objects

One job: hold verified noun cards for source-owned concepts that future agents are likely to change.

## Inputs

- Reference: `../CONTEXT.md`
- Reference: `../_meta/schema.md`
- Source: cited repository files in each card

Do not load every card. Use `_index.md` or `../effects/CONTEXT.md` to pick the task-relevant card set.

## Output

Object cards in cluster folders. Status is declared in each card frontmatter.

## Human Check

Before marking a card `verified`, confirm every load-bearing claim against the cited source and update `verified`.
